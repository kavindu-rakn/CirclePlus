## Compatibility rules and excluded variants

These are constraints for preparing a future proposal, not approval of any mixed baseline:

- Pick an evidenced **visual sub-era/build context** for each client. The broader November 2015–approximately mid-2017 feature envelope permits consideration of features; it does not make their layout, labels or navigation simultaneous. Build identifiers and capture dates stay unknown until verified.
- Keep desktop web, mobile web, native Android, native iOS and tablet variants separate. A phone's Android OS navigation keys do not establish Google+ bottom navigation; Android product destinations do not establish mobile web. C requires a documented adaptation before borrowing native behavior.
- January's announced reduction of whitespace is a compatibility boundary. Do not silently combine a 2015 Home mockup's geometry with a supposedly post-update 2017 Home. No breakpoint/DPR/CSS size is inferred from these rasters or today's browser viewport.
- Keep REF-2017-003's pictured `See all comments` distinct from article `View more comments`; unidentified client/context remains M3. REF-2017-004 is an annotated/static comparison, not proof of tap/drag/zoom sequence or a feed layout.
- Keep the dedicated web notifications center distinct from the cross-product Google-bar panel. [REF-2017-002](web/2017/REF-2017-002-google-bar-limit.md) expressly limits the February panel report; that new panel is excluded as Google+ evidence.
- Exclude classic/pre-redesign, shutdown-era, later enterprise/Currents and modern-library visuals from the consumer baseline. Native drawer Events/Locations do not add v1 scope. The January Events announcement does not approve Events in v1 or change its existing deferral.
- Existing 2015 Android Collections/Communities/profile/settings references remain dated comparisons, not target-era persistence. Generated reports and unclassified images cannot resolve client/build gaps. No approved privacy semantics, icons/licences, motion, token values or new product features follow.
