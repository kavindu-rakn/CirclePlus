# ADR-0001: Historical Target and Fidelity Policy

- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Target the New Google+ generation introduced in November 2015, with a visual anchor around late 2016 / early 2017 and supported features through approximately mid-2017.

Use visually and behaviorally faithful reconstruction where evidence exists. Modernize only invisible technical aspects.

## Consequences

- early Circles-first Google+ is out of scope;
- Material Design 1-era patterns are required;
- references/evidence become part of the implementation workflow;
- some dated UX patterns will intentionally remain.
