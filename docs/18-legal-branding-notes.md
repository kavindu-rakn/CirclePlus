# Legal & Branding Notes

> Non-legal advice. Obtain qualified legal advice if the project becomes public enough for trademark/copyright questions to matter materially.

## Branding stance

The project should use an **independent name, logo, and public identity**.

Use “Google+” descriptively in documentation such as:

> “An unofficial, non-commercial recreation of the Google+ consumer experience as it existed after the 2015 redesign.”

Do not imply endorsement, continuation, or affiliation with Google.

## Avoid

- using `Google+` or `G+` as the product brand
- Google's official G+ logo as the project logo
- domains that imply official Google ownership
- copying proprietary promotional assets without permission
- presenting the app as a reopened official service

## Historical fidelity versus branding

The restoration may need to approximate historical UI structure and color relationships to achieve its purpose, but public project identity should remain clearly independent.

Where an original branded asset is not essential to product behavior, prefer an independently recreated neutral equivalent.

## Repository disclaimer

Recommended public disclaimer:

> This is an unofficial, non-commercial, open-source historical recreation. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Google LLC. Google, Google+, and related marks are trademarks of their respective owner(s).

Final wording should be reviewed before public launch.

## User content/imports

For future Takeout import:

- import only data the user has rights to restore;
- do not mass-republish third-party identities/content simply because it appears in an archive;
- preserve privacy expectations where possible;
- provide deletion/export controls for imported user-owned material.
